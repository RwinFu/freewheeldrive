Scope: the offline scene's *illustrative* joints, not a manufacturing drawing, interference/FEA study, approved coupling selection or confirmation of an installed machine. Reproduce the static and animated checks with `npm test` (`tests/check-scene.mjs` on Three.js r128 and r186, plus `tests/check-mechanics.mjs` and `tests/check-inspection.mjs`).

The viewer remains a **4.008:1** geometric reduction versus the plant reference ideal **32.78:1**. No missing gear stage or second conveyor has been invented. The headless tests establish scene topology and selected bounding-box relationships; bounding-box intersection of a shaft and hub means *connected in the visualization*, not a fit or strength calculation. Browser WebGL appearance and physical as-built connections still require separate inspection and drawings.

## Interface-study geometry versus manufacturing data

The model now draws separate keyed **motor/pump** and **turbine/gearbox** shaft interfaces and collars. Geometry parameters in `FreewheelDriveSpec.model` (metres) are: motor shaft radius 0.047, pump hub bore radius 0.0475; gearbox input shaft radius 0.040, turbine hub bore radius 0.041. The apparent radial clearances (0.5 and 1 mm) are deliberately *visual* and must **not** be used as fits or translated into tolerances. Keys and slots are visual studies; no torque capacity or fretting analysis has been performed. The output bearing study borrows a catalog envelope (90 mm bore / 27 mm mounting clearance / 22 mm candidate bolt) but has no established radial/axial load, life or mounting approval.

To specify production parts, obtain as-built shaft journal diameters/tolerances and material/heat treatment; coupling and gearbox drawings (bore, keyway, shoulders, retaining method); shaft alignment and thermal growth limits; belt tension and resultant radial/axial bearing loads; duty/load spectrum, shock factor and target bearing life. Then check key shear/bearing, shaft fatigue, hub contact pressure, axial retention and bolt preload, bearing dynamic/static capacity and L10 life, and guard/service clearances under load. None of these can be calculated reliably from the single 200 kW reference and ideal ratio alone.
