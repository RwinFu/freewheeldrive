# Published real-drive example vs the concept viewer

A published **real product family** is used as a functional comparison, not as an identification of the user's installed machine. The [Voith T… constant-fill turbo-coupling installation and operating manual, 3626-011000 en, §1.1](https://www.voith.com/corp-en/385_e_3626-011000_en_rev.10.pdf) describes a pump wheel driven by the motor, an opposed turbine wheel connected to the load, an enclosing shell, and hydrodynamic torque transfer without direct mechanical contact between the power-transmitting wheels. It lists filler, fusible and sight-glass service elements. The [SEW X-series industrial bevel-helical gear-unit manual](https://download.sew-eurodrive.com/download/pdf/16931211.pdf) documents gearbox options and service features, but does not identify a gearbox installed here. Neither manual specifies this plant's as-built drive train.

A scene test verifies two wheels and a physical gap. |
| Reduction to the conveyor drum | Gearbox selection is application-specific. | Depicted ≈4.008:1 train vs ideal plant reference ≈32.78:1. | **Unchanged:** no fictitious gear stage. Actual gearbox, fluid slip curve, speed-dependent torque, size and plant coupling selection are **unknown**. |

The start-up differential in the viewer comes from two first-order *display curves*. They do not reproduce Voith performance charts, real torque build-up, fluid temperature or a clutch selection. The motor remains visually free to coast while a removed backstop lets the output run back; it is not an engineered braking or safety prediction. The viewer's V-belt drive and Ø484 mm drum are also still shortened illustrative geometry, not a manufacturing-scale copy of a published system. Real equivalence requires matching nameplates and a dimensioned general arrangement.
