# Catalog-derived service features — not an installed-equipment BOM

The available plant reference specifies duty figures, but does **not** identify the installed fluid-coupling or gearbox type, serial number, orientation, drawing or service options. These catalog manuals are **examples of how actual industrial drives are serviced**, not evidence that a Voith or SEW unit is installed at this plant. No extra gearbox reduction stage, pump, electrical control or load rating is inferred from them.

No catalog dimensions have been imposed on the illustrative service fittings. Their scale and placement are for identification in the exploded view only; maintenance and operation must follow the installed equipment's own instructions. The only currently documented catalog-matched envelope in this project remains the SKF SYJ 90 TF *head-bearing outer proportions, including H2 and catalog bolt/hole diameters* in [reference-audit.md](reference-audit.md), which itself is not proof of a plant bearing selection.
